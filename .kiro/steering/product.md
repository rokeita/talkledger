# TalkLedger — Product

## What it is

TalkLedger is a bookkeeping app for Ghanaian small businesses — market traders, food vendors, small shops — who keep their records in their head, in a notebook, or not at all.

## The problem

Recording a sale means stopping work, finding a pen or opening a spreadsheet. Most sales never get written down, so the trader never really knows if the day made money.

## The idea

Talk instead of type. The trader speaks a sale ("sold 3 bags of rice, 45 cedis") and TalkLedger logs it. MoMo payments arrive as SMS receipts; pasting one files it automatically. The ledger answers one question at a glance: how much did I make today?

## Who it's for

- Market traders and food vendors in Ghana who sell in cash and MoMo.
- Owners who are comfortable speaking but not typing long forms.
- Speakers of Twi and English — often mixed in one sentence.

## Core value

A sale takes five seconds to record: speak, confirm, done. No account, no internet needed to record.

## Non-goals (MVP)

- No bank or MoMo API integration — receipts come from pasted SMS text only.
- No multi-user accounts, no cloud sync, no tax filing.
- No full Twi speech recognition — the browser's speech engine handles English; Twi is supported through a keyword dictionary for common items and numbers, with typed fallback.
- No inventory management beyond the sale entries themselves.
